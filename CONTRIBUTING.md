# Contributing

> [!CAUTION]
> **Offered without support.** Bloem Admin is provided as-is, with no support of any kind. Issues are turned off, questions go unanswered, and pull requests are not accepted: they are closed without review. You are free to fork it under the AGPL v3.

## Contributions

Contributions are not accepted. Pull requests are closed without review, and issues are turned off.

## Forking

Bloem Admin is licensed under the [GNU AGPL v3](LICENSE). You may fork it, change it and distribute it under those terms.
The [README](README.md) explains how to build your own copy. If you distribute a modified build, give it a different name
and icon, and your own bundle identifiers, as described in [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md).

## Security

Security vulnerabilities may be reported privately. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
